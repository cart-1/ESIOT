EXP1b:

i. Sample of Assembly language program:

ORG 0H
MOV R5, #25H
MOV R7,#34H
MOV A, #0
ADD A, R5
ADD A, R7
ADD A, #12H
HERE:SJMP HERE
END

ii. Multiply 25 by 10 using the technique of repeated addition:

MOV A, #0
MOV R2, #10
AGAIN:ADD A, #25
DJNZ R2, AGAIN
MOV R5,A

iii. Program to add first 10 natural number:

MOV A,#0
MOV R2, #10
MOV R0, #0
AGAIN:INC R0
ADD A,R0
DJNZ R2, AGAIN
MOV 46H, A

iv. Program to load the accumulator with the value of 55H and complement the ACC
700 times:

MOV A,#55H
MOV R3,#10
NEXT:MOV R2,#70
AGAIN:CPL A
DJNZ R2,AGAIN
DJNZ R3,NEXT

v. Multiply ECH by 25H using the technique of repeated addition:

MOV R1,#0
MOV A,#0
MOV R0,#25H
AGAIN: ADD A,#0ECH
JNC HERE
INC R1
HERE: DJNZ R0,AGAIN
MOV R0,A
